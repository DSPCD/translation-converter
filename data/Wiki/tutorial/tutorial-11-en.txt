Instructions for Basic Controls
{$Text|fontsize=14;linespacing=1.1;textalignment=0,1;color=#FFFFFF52;material=UI/Materials/widget-text-alpha-5x-thick;margins=20,20,20,20}
Control the mecha using the [$LKEY_1;/$LKEY_2;/$LKEY_3;/$LKEY_4;] direction keys or give a move command to the mecha by clicking [$LKEY_5;] at a blank space.

Press the [$LKEY_8;] key to make the mecha jump.

The mecha will automatically switch to Drift Mode when it encounters water. It will consume more energy in the Drift Mode compared to moving on land.

{$Text|fontsize=14;linespacing=1.1;textalignment=0,1;color=#FFFFFF52;material=UI/Materials/widget-text-alpha-5x-thick;margins=20,20,20,20}
After unlocking "Drive Engine 1" and gaining the ability to fly, <color="#FD965ECC">press the [$LKEY_9;] key twice</color> to fly the mecha. Then make the mecha fly at low altitude using the [$LKEY_1;], [$LKEY_2;], [$LKEY_3;], and [$LKEY_4;] keys. In Flight Mode, the mecha's speed will be boosted by 150% and more energy will be consumed.

During the flight, increase the flying altitude by pressing the <color="#FD965ECC">[$LKEY_10;]</color> key and reduce the flying altitude or land the mecha by pressing the <color="#FD965ECC">[$LKEY_13;]</color> key.

After unlocking "Drive Engine 2" and gaining the ability to cruise, <color="#FD965ECC">press and hold the [$LKEY_1;] and [$LKEY_10;] keys</color> in Flight Mode to make the mecha continuously fly upward. After reaching a certain altitude, the mecha will enter Cruise Mode.
